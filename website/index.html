What it does

Small features, deeply considered. Nothing speculative.

Faces

On-device recognition with InsightFace models. Group photos by person, name them once, browse a face's history.

Albums

Suggested automatically for trips and events from time and place clusters. Accept, edit, ignore.

Memories

"This day, three years ago." A quiet rediscovery surface that never asks for your data in exchange.

Map

Every geotagged photo, plotted. Click a cluster to drill into a place; tiles cached per-drive.

Insights

Shooting heatmap, top people, top locations, monthly cadence. Click any cell to see the photos behind it.

Search

Unified across people, albums, places, and photos. Date filters. Per-place / per-person scopes. Fast on hundreds of thousands of photos.

Why offline?

Your photos are personal. Smriti keeps them that way.

No upload

Photos stay on your drive. Smriti never copies, syncs, or transmits image data — anywhere, ever.

No account

No sign-up, no login, no email. Open the app, point it at a folder, done.

No telemetry

No analytics, no crash reporting, no "anonymous usage statistics." The only optional outbound call is a once-a-day version check, and it's off by default.
